The Table

A small Italian kitchen
in the heart of Higashiyama

A few quiet steps from Gion, Gion Asakura is an intimate restaurant where Italian technique meets Japanese produce. Handmade pastas and seasonal plates are cooked simply and finished with the restraint of Kyoto.

The room is small and the pace unhurried — an evening here is meant to be lingered over, course by course, glass by glass.

Buonasera, and welcome to Gion.
