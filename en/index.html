Concept

A small Italian kitchen in Higashiyama

A few quiet steps from Gion, Gion Asakura is an intimate counter restaurant where Italian technique meets Japanese produce. We keep seating limited so you can enjoy a leisurely evening — linger course by course, glass by glass.

Guest Reviews

★★★★★

At the end of 2025 I visited Kyoto with my family. This was my mom’s favorite restaurant — she said it was the best meal we had there. There weren’t Korean reviews, but I highly recommend it. I rarely write reviews, but I really wanted to share this place.

I don’t speak Japanese, so we communicated with a translator; the chef was so warm. When I asked what was in the fruit salad, he even brought out a persimmon to show me.

Because it was the end of the year we ordered some drinks too — they were fantastic!

I especially loved the tuna cheek with avocado; my mom said it was the best dish she’d had even in Korea.

The spicy tomato pasta that came last was perfectly seasoned and delicious — I was already full but still wanted more.

What moved me most was that the chef kept coming out to greet us until we left the alley.

I’ll definitely book again next time I’m in Kyoto. Happy New Year!

gonana · Google

★★★★★

Blew us away. We made a last minute (day of) reservation, hoping this 7 seater restaurant would seat the 3 of us. Expected Italian but it was more fusion. Very little English spoken but why speak when you can communicate through the wildly delicious food?! Chef Azakura brought us 8 amazing courses, perfectly seasoned and beautifully presented. He only had one assistant but he did almost all the cooking while his assistant served drinks and anything not related to the actual cooking. Chef Azakura handled his 7 guests with poise and ease, even though it must have been a juggle. It happened to be my mom’s 80th birthday that evening and he brought her dessert with a candle. It was a prix fixe dinner for ¥16,000. We were absolutely delighted and recommended it to some friends in town who went the very next night. This restaurant is tucked in a little alley, it can be difficult to find, just rely on GPS and the photos posted here in the reviews of the front of the restaurant. If you do go, tell him “Daphne” sent you. Enjoy!!

Valerie Ang-Chamorro · Google
